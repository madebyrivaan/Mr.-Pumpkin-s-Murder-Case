| Investigation Phase |
| --- |
| **Suspect 1: Lady Lemon (Romantic and Financial Motive)**<br/><br/>- **Motive:** Following her husband's passing, Lady Lemon wished to maintain her lavish lifestyle without working. She attempted to seduce Mr. Pumpkin and proposed merging their company shares. However, Mr. Pumpkin insulted her as a scrawny leech and flatly rejected the business proposal.<br/>- **Alibi / Testimony:** Claims she merely stopped by the study room to congratulate Mr. Pumpkin on the anniversary.<br/>- **Unmasked Through Interrogation:** Inspector Garlic points out the ring on the floor. Crying bitter tears, Lady Lemon admits she confessed her feelings and offered the share merger, but after being humiliated, she threw her diamond ring at his face in a rage and left.<br/>- **Deduction:** She clearly has a motive, but the heavy iron garden shovel is far too heavy for a delicate, high society lady to wield during a struggle, and no signs of a physical struggle were found on the victim's body => indicating a strong individual must have committed the crime.<br/>- **Link to Other Suspects:** Accused by Onion of receiving the diamond ring as a gift from Mr. Pumpkin and being someone with romantic feelings for and using romantic feelings to manipulate, the victim |
| **Suspect 2: Mr. Chili (The Framed Suspect)**<br/><br/>- **Motive:** Mr. Chili violated banking protocols to approve a massive loan for Mr. Pumpkin. When Mr. Pumpkin defaulted on the debt, Chili faced career ruin. Worse, Mr. Pumpkin threatened to expose Chili's corruption to the authorities.<br/>- **Alibi / Testimony:** Claims he only went into the room to smoke cigars and discuss business with the victim.<br/>- **Unmasked Through Interrogation:** Inspector Garlic taunts Chili regarding his upcoming bank audit. Chili loses control, his skin turning bright red with rage. He accidentally blurts out: "I only picked up that shovel to threaten him! The shovel he usually uses to tend plants in the room! But I threw it on the floor and walked out!"<br/>- **Red Herring Trap:** Chili's fingerprints cover the shovel handle, his cigar ash lies on the desk, combined with a desperate financial motive. All evidence points to Chili as the violent killer.<br/>- **Link to Other Suspects:** Accused by Onion regarding the loan partnership between Chili and the victim. Accused by Lemon who claims she saw Chili exit the room with an extremely angry expression. |
| **Suspect 3: Mr. Onion (Opportunist and Underworld Boss)**<br/><br/>- **Motive:** Mr. Pumpkin held Onion's secret smuggling contract and used it for extortion, threatening to turn Onion into a signature machine for his money laundering schemes.<br/>- **Alibi / Testimony:** Asserts he only socialized in the party hall all night, never stepping foot inside Mr. Pumpkin's study room.<br/>- **Unmasked Through Interrogation (Unpeeling Each Layer):**<br/>    - **Layer 1:** Garlic highlights that the sleeve of Onion's expensive suit carries the scent of machine oil mixed with the distinctive plant sap from the victim's desk drawer.<br/>    - **Layer 2:** Onion confesses he slipped into the room after Chili left to steal back the smuggling contract. He saw Mr. Pumpkin already collapsed on the desk with faint breathing, so he quietly stole the document and withdrew.<br/>- **Deduction:** Onion is an opportunistic and cunning individual. He was present at the crime scene right when the victim was dying, Because of his bias against the police, he refuses to cooperate with their investigation. Plus, working in the underworld makes him want to avoid getting involved with the cops even more<br/>- **Link to Other Suspects:** As someone operating in the underworld, none of his "friends" in the group possessed clear information about him |
| **The True Mastermind - LADY LEMON**<br/><br/>- **Timeline:** Onion stole the document and fled while Mr. Pumpkin was unconscious. Lady Lemon slipped back into the study room immediately after Onion left.<br/>- **True Murder Method:&#32;**Lady Lemon did not rely on physical strength. When Mr. Pumpkin insulted her (pushing her vanity, arrogance, and flattery-accustomed nature past their breaking point) she secretly dropped highly concentrated lemon juice into his champagne glass. The violent reaction from the extreme acidity triggered fatal internal toxic shock, causing severe pain that ultimately induced a fatal heart attack (exacerbated by his pre existing condition)<br/>- **Evidence & Deduction:&#32;**Inspector Garlic accidentally smelled an unusual aroma in the victim's champagne glass that differed distinctly from the flavor of the remaining wine inside the bottle**&#32;**-> the only person capable of producing such a subtle, natural scent (not strong as Onion or hot as Chili) was Lady Lemon. Lacking the physical strength to overpower the victim in a direct confrontation, she resorted to poisoning as her method of murder.<br/>- **Staging the Scene:&#32;**Spotting Mr. Pumpkin in critical condition collapsed on the floor from the poison, Lady Lemon noticed Chili's discarded shovel lying nearby. She used her foot to press forcefully on the shovel blade, driving it deep into the victim's body. Because there were no signs of a physical struggle, she successfully staged a fake violent murder to shift all suspicion onto the hot - tempered Chili |

| Game Flow + Core gameplay loop |
| --- |
| **Game Flow**<br/><br/>1.Character Introduction & Briefing Phase (name + age + job + status)<br/><br/>2. Crime Scene Investigation Phase: Gather Clues ( ring, ash, a page of document) -> add to inventory ( notebook)<br/>3. Interrogation Phase (dialog choice): interviews each suspect by choosing specific questions -> suspect answers reveal different portions of the narrative based on the player's questioning path -> add to inventory ( notebook)<br/>4. End-Game Phase<br/><br/>- Lose Condition (Insufficient Evidence / Wrong Choice): NPCs have the right to claim fatigue or discomfort with the questions -> preventing the player from asking further questions -> player fails to gather enough evidence -> Final screen appears, forcing a choice among the 3 suspects -> picks incorrectly -> Game Over<br/>- Win: after the interview, full information is gathered -> add in the Inventory -> makes deductions -> Final screen appears, offering a choice among the 3 suspects -> picks correctly -> Solve the case (maybe end cutscene showing an animation of the suspect killing the victim) |
| **Core gameplay loop**<br/>Investigation Phase -> Interrogation -> Deduction -> Final Verdict & Ending |
